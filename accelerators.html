Our Accelerator Portfolio

1. Data Onboarding Service

Rapid, governed onboarding of data sources into your platform

We help you onboard new data sources micro-batch, batch or streaming, using standardized ingestion patterns, validation checks, and data contracts.

What's included

  • Source assessment & ingestion blueprint
  • Batch, CDC, and streaming pipelines
  • Schema enforcement & data contracts
  • Data quality checks & validation rules
  • Metadata, lineage, and documentation
  • Built-in compliance & approvals

Outcomes

  • New data sources onboarded in days
  • Reduced pipeline failures and rework
  • Trusted, analytics- and AI-ready datasets

2. User Onboarding Service

Secure, scalable access for analytics, engineering, and AI users

Enable teams to access data safely and productively from day one.

What's included

  • User personas & access patterns
  • SSO integration
  • Environment-aware access (Dev/Test/Prod)
  • Analytics & AI workspace setup
  • Usage monitoring & audit trails

Outcomes

  • Reduced decision latency
  • Faster analyst and engineer productivity
  • Faster access & reduced access-related incidents
  • Clear ownership and accountability

3. Role Onboarding Service

Enterprise-grade RBAC and access governance

We design and implement role-based access models that scale across teams, tools, and data domains.

What's included

  • Role taxonomy (Admin, Engineer, Analyst, AI Agent & functional roles etc.)
  • Group-to-role mapping
  • Least-privilege access design
  • Automated provisioning & deprovisioning
  • Audit-ready role documentation

Outcomes

  • Consistent access across platforms
  • Improved security posture
  • Simplified compliance and audits

4. Data FinOps Service

Control and optimize data platform costs continuously

Turn cloud and data spend into a managed, visible, and optimized function.

What's included

  • Cost baselining & usage attribution
  • Warehouse and pipeline cost analysis
  • Chargeback / showback models
  • Optimization recommendations
  • Cost guardrails & alerts

Outcomes

  • Reduced warehouse and compute spend
  • Cost tied to teams, projects, and value
  • Predictable monthly spend

5. Synthetic Data Generation

Safe, scalable data for development, testing, and AI

Generate realistic, compliant synthetic datasets that mirror production data—without exposing sensitive information.

What's included

  • PII classification & masking strategy
  • Synthetic data generation pipelines
  • Statistical and behavioral fidelity checks
  • AI/ML-ready datasets
  • Reusable data templates

Outcomes

  • Faster development and testing cycles
  • Zero exposure of sensitive data
  • Improved AI model training and validation

6. Operating Model Accelerator

Define how data actually runs in your organization

We help you design and implement a clear operating model across people, process, and technology.

What's included

  • Data org structure & ownership model
  • Platform vs domain responsibilities
  • Intake, prioritization, and delivery workflows
  • SLAs, SLOs, and support models
  • Governance and decision forums

Outcomes

  • Clear accountability and ownership
  • Faster decision-making
  • Sustainable, scalable data operations
