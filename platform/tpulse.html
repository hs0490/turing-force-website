TPulse. Powered by Intelligence.

Interactive answers to your business questions across portfolios. Ask a question. Get a governed answer.

Ask TPulse See live demo

Why TPulse

TPulse turns portfolio data into interactive, agentic answers—backlog, sprints, risk, dependencies, cost—without moving your data.

⚡

Decide faster

Instant, conversational answers instead of hunting across tools.

🎯

Actionable by design

Ask follow-ups, then trigger agents to update, assign, notify, or create work items.

🛡️

Governed & explainable

RBAC, data contracts, audit trails, and per-agent budgets ensure safe, traceable responses.

🏠

Runs where your data lives

Context is streamed under policy; no bulk data migration.

What TPulse does

💬

Interactive Q&A

Natural-language questions about delivery status, blockers, velocity, cost, and risk.

🧠

Context reasoning

Hybrid retrieval (BM25 + vectors) + workspace memory for precise, on-topic answers.

🔗

Answer provenance

Inline evidence and links back to source records; show "why" and "where it came from."

⚡

Follow-up actions

Convert answers into actions via agents (assign owner, update status, create ticket, post summary).

👥

Multirole views

Responses adapt to AI-Exec-Viewer, AI-Product-Owner, AI-Scrum-Master, AI-Developer, AI-Auditor.

How TPulse works

Inside the TuringForce platform

1

Connect & Contract (TuringNexus)

Bring sources and actions under data contracts (allowlists, redaction, TTLs).

2

Design Agents (TuringCanvas)

Optional: compose helper agents for summaries, risk scans, or cost rollups.

3

Orchestrate (TuringFlow)

Schedule refreshes or run on-demand; maintain fresh, governed context windows.

4

Guardrails (TuringForge)

Compile/validate prompts, enforce RBAC, audit, and cost/token budgets.

5

Ask TPulse

Users ask questions; TPulse retrieves governed context, reasons, cites sources, and can trigger follow-ups.

Core capabilities

Portfolio Answers

Readiness, velocity, scope confidence, risk heatmaps, dependency impact, cost signals.

Blocker Radar

Detect items idle beyond thresholds; surface owners, dependencies, and next best actions.

Status in Seconds

Generate exec-ready updates with deltas, trends, and confidence notes.

Cost Insights

Attribute spend to agents, teams, or workstreams; flag anomalies and right-size opportunities.

Drill-down & "Why"

Expand an answer to see evidence, filters, and the reasoning path.

Example prompts

"What threatens this sprint goal and who owns each blocker?"

"Which stories fail Definition of Ready for next sprint?"

"Show velocity trend and forecasted capacity for the next two sprints."

"What's the cost per completed story this month and top drivers?"

"Summarize cross-team dependencies likely to slip this release."
