What we do

We partner with teams to apply proven software engineering rigor to data engineering, embedding standards, automation, testing, and CI/CD so data platforms scale reliably from design to production.

Services

🏗️

Data Platform & Architecture

Modern data stack and reference architectures (Snowflake/Databricks/Iceberg, etc.). Landing zones, environments (Dev/Staging/Prod), deployment patterns.

📥

Ingestion & Pipeline Engineering

Batch + Streaming ingestion, CDC patterns, Orchestration, Error handling. Reliability: Retries, Idempotency, SLAs/SLOs.

🔄

Data Modeling & Transformation

Curated datasets for analytics and ML/AI & Agentic use-cases. dbt-style transformation standards, semantic modeling, documentation.

✅

Data Quality, Validation & Observability

Automated quality checks and validation to ensure consistency/accuracy. Monitoring, lineage, anomaly detection, and incident playbooks.

🔐

Governance, Security & Privacy

Access controls, auditability, policy guardrails, PII handling. Platform-ready patterns for compliance and enterprise rollout.

💰

Cost Optimization (FinOps for Data & Platform)

Warehouse and pipeline cost visibility, chargeback/showback. Rightsizing + scheduling + workload efficiency recommendations.
